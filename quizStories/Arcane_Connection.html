(- one particular night after a few months -)
The shaman was resting on his hammock on his house's porch. Swaying lightly and enjoying the gentle breeze. Suddenly, he felt something in his corner of sight. As he tried to focus upon it, the thing disappear. Something like this doesn't bother him as much. Furthermore, he do have some weird connection with the otherworldly. But his heart have this passing thought of Iqbal. Is the thing he saw connected to him? Or is it something random? Suddenly, his phone rang. It is from Iqbal.
"hey my man, how are you?" the shaman casually greet Iqbal on the line "what's the matter?". The other side of the line seems quite and no one seems to respond. The line suddenly hung up. The shaman look at his phone and try to see if the on that called really is Iqbal. The logs show it is Iqbal. But, weirdly, it is a miscall. It is like, the shaman never answered the phone. The shaman scratch his head that is not itchy. "technologies these days" he wave it off and back to his night scenery enjoy session.
But deep down, he felt something is wrong. Something is off. His heart tell him that there is something that might happen to Iqbal. Not particularly bad, but, something. He prayed to Allah for guidance and to give him answers to his uneasiness. He wipe his face and continue his stargazing. The night is quite with cricket chirping and owl hooting. Nothing much. Then again, his phone rang. Now it is from a number he doesn't recognized. He answer it nonetheless
"hello, who is this?" the shaman grew suspicious. "h-hello, is this shaman?" the person on the other end sounded like he is panting. "yeah, yeah. This is the shaman." The shaman answer nonetheless "who is this?". The guy from the other side sounded relieved and start to calm down. "I am iqbal's assistant. I need your help. Iqbal is missing and something attacked me. Like, mythically". The shaman was taken aback
(- a noon after Iqbal went missing for some days -)
The shaman was enjoying his noon breeze. The sun is up high but the shaded area on the porch where is sit is perfect for the afternoon daydreaming. There he was in his usual garment; a kain gombang and a plain shirt. "where are you, Iqbal? Where did you go?" he throw is eyesight far into the distance.
All this time he knows Iqbal and have him healed the traditional way, many of weird things happened. It is just another normal Tuesday for him though. But this time it felt weird. He know that he is in grief of his lost friend. He is in deep stress after the job situation. And definitely there is something happening,,, after his arwah grandfather passing. One ujian after the other.
But nonetheless, this is just a part of his journey that Allah have gave him. Maybe there is something for him throughout the journey. The shaman grew drowsier as the small waterway near his house sung a beautiful afternoon lullaby. And not long after, he drifted away to his nap.
